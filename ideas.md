# Farm2Home visual direction

## Direction
**Harvest-led modern local food startup** — a warm, tactile marketplace that blends neighborhood farms and fresh produce with the clarity of a contemporary commerce product. The Mira-Bhayandar setting should feel local without borrowing unrelated corporate or foreign-farm branding.

## Design movement and principles
Use modern editorial food photography and a clean startup interface. Balance freshness and trust with restrained polish: confident typography, generous breathing room, clear price/action hierarchy, warm neutral backgrounds, accessible contrast, and grounded details such as place labels, farmer names, harvest dates, and delivery windows. Avoid a monochrome green page, generic Bootstrap treatments, excessive gradients, or repeating identical card sections.

## Color philosophy
Preserve the supplied palette exactly as the design tokens: primary green `#1F6B45`, deep green `#123C2A`, light green `#E8F5EC`, cream `#FFF9EF`, warm beige `#F4EBDD`, turmeric accent `#F2A93B`, body ink `#183027`, muted green-gray `#6B7C73`, and white. Use cream and white for calm reading areas, deep green only for high-contrast anchors, light green for farm/product surfaces, and turmeric sparingly for ratings or small highlights.

## Layout paradigm and signature elements
A sticky translucent navigation, split editorial hero with a photographic produce image, overlaid freshness/delivery proof tags, alternating full-width section backgrounds, an illustrated five-stage route from discovery to doorstep, generous product imagery, strong price labels, and a standout three-tier subscription selector. Dashboards use a dark-green side rail with bright content canvas, compact metrics and clear charts; on mobile the side rail collapses into a touch-friendly navigation drawer and tables scroll intentionally.

## Interaction philosophy
Make actions legible and honest. Add-to-cart, checkout, subscription controls, delivery slots, reviews, approvals, and product management should show loading, success, empty and error states. Keep destructive actions explicit, preserve entered form values on recoverable errors, provide visible keyboard focus, and do not imply real payment, machine learning, or live GPS. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Animation and typography
Use Poppins or Inter, large editorial headings, comfortable line lengths, and distinct numeric typography for prices/statistics. Use brief fade/slide reveals, gentle card lift, subtle image crop zoom and small cart feedback only; keep all motion optional under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Brand essence, voice and mark
**Essence:** nearby farms, dependable freshness, transparent delivery. **Voice:** welcoming, practical, specific, and optimistic—never make unsupported health or sustainability claims. Wordmark: “Farm2Home” paired with a simple seed/leaf mark. The square project icon uses the same seedling/field identity in two or three broad flat shapes, no baked text, full-bleed cream background, and a strong silhouette that remains recognizable at favicon size. Signature brand color: `#1F6B45`.

## Asset placements
1. A unique 1:1 opaque Farm2Home project icon: full-bleed warm cream background and a centered, flat seedling/field emblem using deep and primary green; no text or badge shape.
2. A 3:2 landscape hero image: editorial, realistic close-up of freshly harvested Indian vegetables and herbs arranged in a reusable market basket at a small coastal Maharashtra farm, soft golden morning light, natural foliage, earthy cream and green palette, image-weight positioned right with negative space on left edge for crop. No text, logo, watermark, or stock-photo aesthetic.
